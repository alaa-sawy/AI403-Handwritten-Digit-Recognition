## 2. Technical issues and resolutions

1. **Duplicate image:** Detected one exact duplicate among the 5,009 official training entries and excluded it from the usable development pool. The source archive was not modified.
2. **Leakage prevention:** Confirmed that no exact image SHA-256 overlaps were found between the frozen training and validation sets, or between the official training and test image sets. Writer independence could not be verified because writer identifiers were unavailable; perceptual near-duplicates were not assessed.
3. **Baseline prediction errors:** The CNN+CTC baseline frequently produced short predictions, particularly `10`, resulting in substantial deletion errors. These patterns were analyzed without presenting unverified causal explanations as established findings.
4. **Evaluation protocol:** Used only the frozen validation split for baseline tuning, model selection and error analysis; official test labels remained sealed during M1 experiments.
5. **Reproducibility:** Preserved dataset split manifests, configuration files, training logs, validation metrics, curves and the three final model checkpoints in the team repository.
